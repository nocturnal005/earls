FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does bespoke picture framing take?

Most bespoke frames are completed within 7–10 working days. Express turnaround is available for urgent orders — please contact us to discuss your deadline.

Can I frame my photo online?

Yes. Use our Frame My Photo configurator to upload your image, choose your frame, mount, and glass, then order directly. We deliver across the UK.

What types of framing does Earl's offer?

We offer bespoke picture framing, canvas stretching, shadow boxes for memorabilia, custom picture mounts, fine art Giclée printing, glass cut to size, and frame restoration.

Where is Earl's Frames London located?

We are located at Unit 49, Rye Lane, Peckham, London SE15 5BY. Open Monday to Friday 9am–6pm and Saturday 10am–4pm.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. We offer standard delivery (10–12 working days) and express delivery (3–5 working days) across the UK for online orders.

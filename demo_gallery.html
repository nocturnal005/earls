Interactive Gallery Prototypes

Play with the three sections below. Notice how each handles hover states, focus, and depth differently without causing the layout to stutter.

1. The Infinite Cinematic Marquee

Hover anywhere to slow/pause the track. Hover a specific image to bring it into sharp focus while the rest fade away. No jittering margins.

2. Mouse-Tracked Parallax & Depth of Field

Move your mouse quickly across the images. The cards will literally physically tilt to follow your cursor in 3D space. Intense depth-of-field blur draws focus.

3. 3D Interactive Coverflow

A physical, museum-like browsing experience in a curved plane. Items pushed to the sides rotate away from the viewer.

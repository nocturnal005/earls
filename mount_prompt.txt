SYSTEM PROMPT — PICTURE MOUNT CONFIGURATOR PANEL
You are building the Mounting section of the Earl's Framing website configurator.
The configurator is a right-side panel that sits alongside a live frame preview.
The panel is approximately 400px wide. It shows mount options as a tile grid.
The customer selects one tile. It applies to the frame preview. The price updates.
One button at the bottom: APPLY CHANGE.
━━ PANEL STRUCTURE ━━
The panel heading is: MOUNTING
Below the heading: a scrollable tile grid, 3 columns wide.
Below the grid: 'Price incl. VAT, excl. shipping' and the running total.
At the bottom: a full-width APPLY CHANGE button in Earl's Framing red (#C0392B).
━━ TILE DESIGN ━━
Each tile is a card with three zones (top to bottom):
  1. COLOUR SWATCH — fills the top 60% of the tile with the mount hex colour.
     Shows mount type (small bold text) and ref code (small italic) at the bottom of the swatch.
     Text colour: white (#FFFFFF) on dark swatches (hex < #909090), dark (#222222) on light swatches.
  2. NAME AREA — white background.
     Line 1: colour name, bold, navy (#1A3C5C).
     Line 2: popularFor string, small italic grey (#555555).
  3. PRICE BAND — coloured background matching the mount type colour.
     Shows: '+£X.XX' in white bold, and 'FATG Level X' in small light text.
Mount type colours (for the price band and any type labels):
  White Core:   #1E5C8A
  Black Core:   #333333
  Conservation: #1A6B3C
  Extra Thick:  #5A3C8A
  Museum:       #8A5A1A
  Suede:        #7A3C5C
Selected tile state: add a solid 2px border in Earl's Framing red (#C0392B) around the entire tile.
Hover state: slight scale(1.02) and a soft box-shadow.
Default (no mount): first tile (Pure White, White Core) is pre-selected on load.
━━ TILE GROUPS ━━
Tiles are organised into 6 groups. Display a small group label above each group of tiles.
Group labels are left-aligned, small caps or small bold text, in a muted colour (#888888).
Groups scroll vertically inside the panel. Do not paginate.
The 6 groups and their order:
  1. Whites & Neutrals
  2. Greys
  3. Black Core
  4. Colour Accents
  5. Conservation
  6. Premium
━━ PRICING LOGIC ━━
The add-on price shown on each tile is per sheet at A1 size, inc. VAT.
Formula: £1.485 × gradeMultiplier × sizeMultiplier × 3
The default size is A1 (sizeMultiplier = 1.00).
If your configurator supports multiple sizes, recalculate using the size multipliers
from the full price guide. Do not hardcode prices for non-A1 sizes.
